You’re in. Here’s what this place is.

A curated library of AI prompts for pathology education — learning, teaching, and the documentation around teaching events. It started as a workshop companion. The goal is for it to grow into the place pathology educators come to share what works.

What you can do here: browse 88 battle-tested prompts across three pillars, read tutorials on writing good prompts and picking the right LLM, comment and upvote on entries that worked for you, submit your own prompts for review, and use the floating Feedback button (bottom-right of every page) to flag bugs or suggest features.

The vision: a Reddit-style community of practice around AI in pathology education — where pathologists, residents, fellows, and program directors learn from what each other have tried, instead of each re-inventing prompts independently.

Three pillars, organized by use

Pick the pillar that matches what you’re trying to do right now. Every prompt is structured the same way — what it does, when to use it, the prompt itself, expected output, common failure modes, and the human verification step that must come after.

The three pillars

Pillar I

Self-Education

Concept explanation at level, self-quizzing, MCQ generation, diagnostic algorithm walkthroughs, board prep scaffolding, paper critique, multimodal practice on photomicrographs and SPEP traces.
