## How to cite this library

If you use this library to inform your own teaching, residency program design, or scholarly work, please cite it. Suggested format below; adapt to your venue's required style.

### APA 7

> Baba, O. Z. (2026). *AI in pathology education — Companion library: A practical framework across learning, teaching, and educational operations* [Educational resource]. Henry Ford Health, Department of Pathology. https://github.com/omarzbaba/AI_Pathology_Education

### AMA

> Baba OZ. *AI in Pathology Education — Companion Library: A Practical Framework Across Learning, Teaching, and Educational Operations*. Henry Ford Health, Department of Pathology; 2026. Accessed [date]. https://github.com/omarzbaba/AI_Pathology_Education

### Vancouver

> Baba OZ. AI in pathology education — companion library: a practical framework across learning, teaching, and educational operations [Internet]. Detroit (MI): Henry Ford Health, Department of Pathology; 2026 [cited YYYY MMM DD]. Available from: https://github.com/omarzbaba/AI_Pathology_Education

### Plain text (for slide decks, posters)

> Baba OZ (2026). AI in Pathology Education — Companion Library. API Summit 2026.

## Citing a specific prompt or worked example

If you cite a specific prompt or worked example, include the path within the library:

> Baba OZ (2026). *Self-quizzing through SPEP interpretation* [worked example]. In: AI in Pathology Education — Companion Library, Pillar I. https://github.com/omarzbaba/AI_Pathology_Education

## Citing the Companion App Builder

The standalone Claude Code prompt in Pillar III has been used to build at least one production event companion site (this one). If you build a site with it and publish or share that work:

> Baba OZ (2026). *Companion App Builder* [standalone Claude Code prompt]. In: AI in Pathology Education — Companion Library, Pillar III. https://github.com/omarzbaba/AI_Pathology_Education

## Let me know if you use it

The author is preparing a manuscript on structured AI literacy in pathology training, and one of its evidence bases is how the community uses this library. If you adopt prompts at your institution, use the worked examples in resident education, or build a companion site with the Companion App Builder, please drop the author a short note.

Useful information to include:

- Your role and institution
- Which prompts you used and for what
- Anything you adapted, fixed, or improved
- Whether you'd consent to being acknowledged in the manuscript (you can decline — the request is genuinely opt-in)

Contact: Omar Z. Baba, MD — Henry Ford Health, Department of Pathology, Clinical Pathology & Informatics. Reach the author through Henry Ford Health's pathology department directory or via the GitHub repository for this library.
