These terms govern your use of the AI in Pathology Education Companion Library, including the prompt library, the submit-a-prompt form, comments, and votes.

By accessing the library, submitting a prompt, posting a comment, or voting on a prompt, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use those features.

## Acceptable use

You may use the library to:

- Read, copy, and adapt prompts for your own pathology education work
- Submit your own original prompts for inclusion in the library
- Comment substantively on prompts (what worked, what didn't, refinements you found, questions about specific applications)
- Upvote prompts you found useful

You may NOT use the library to:

- Submit prompts, comments, or votes containing patient health information (PHI) of any kind, identifiable or de-identified
- Submit copyrighted material you don't have the right to share under CC BY 4.0
- Post comments containing hostile, harassing, or unprofessional content
- Post commercial advertising, promotional spam, or off-topic content
- Impersonate another person (the author, an institution, or any other person)
- Attempt to bypass moderation, identity gates, or technical protections
- Use automated tools to vote, comment, or submit at scale

## Moderation policy

**Comments and submissions are pre-moderated.** Nothing you post appears publicly until the author has reviewed and approved it. The author's review timeline is typically 2-3 weeks; high-volume periods may be longer.

The author may:

- **Approve** content that meets these terms and the library's quality standards
- **Hide** previously-approved content if it later turns out to violate these terms
- **Reject** submissions that don't fit the library or don't meet the quality bar
- **Decline to engage** with comments that don't merit a response

The author's moderation decisions are final. The author is not obligated to explain individual moderation decisions, though they will try to provide reasoning when it's useful for the contributor.

## Comments and votes — public display

Approved comments display publicly:

- Your name
- Your institution / affiliation (if you provided one)
- Your comment text
- An approximate timestamp ("3 days ago")

Your email address is stored privately and is NOT displayed publicly.

Vote counts are public; individual votes are not attributed publicly.

## Account-equivalent and termination

The library doesn't have user accounts in the traditional sense. Identity is established by the email address you provide when interacting with the library.

If you violate these terms, the author may:

- Reject future submissions, comments, or votes from your email address
- Hide previously-approved content associated with your email address
- Block your email from interacting with the library

Repeated or severe violations may result in your contributions being removed from public display, though they remain in the audit trail as required by the library's data retention practices.

## Email and notifications

If you submit a prompt or post a comment, the author may email you regarding:

- The status of your submission (approved / needs revision / rejected)
- Clarifying questions about your submission
- Notification when a comment you posted has been approved or hidden

The library does NOT send marketing emails, newsletters, or unsolicited messages. The only emails you'll receive are direct correspondence from the author about your specific submission or comment.
