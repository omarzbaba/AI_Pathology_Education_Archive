## Community-contributed prompts

The AI in Pathology Education Companion Library accepts community contributions of prompts. Every accepted contribution is credited to the contributor here.

If you've used a prompt from the library and would like to share your own, use the [Submit a prompt](/submit.html) form. Dr. Baba reviews submissions personally; you should hear back within 2-3 weeks.

## How contributions are reviewed

Submissions are evaluated against the same standards as the existing library:

- **Useful and specific to pathology education** — not generic AI prompts dressed up as pathology
- **Has the seven-section structure** (what it does, when, the prompt, expected output, failure modes, verification, best model) — or close enough that we can edit it into shape with minor changes
- **Honest about limitations** — prompts that overpromise are returned for revision
- **No PHI** — patient material is never accepted
- **Original** — copies or close paraphrases of existing library prompts are not accepted; meaningful variations are
- **The submitter has the right to share** under CC BY 4.0

Most rejections happen because the prompt is too generic (could apply to any field, not specifically pathology) or too specific (institutional workflow unique to one place).

## Contributor list

*Accepted contributions will be listed here with the contributor's name, institution (if provided), and the prompts they contributed. This page populates as the first community contributions are approved.*

### How to be listed here

1. [Submit your prompt](/submit.html) using the structured form
2. Wait for review (2-3 weeks)
3. If accepted, your prompt is added to the library with your name on it, and your name appears here

## Attribution norms

When citing a community-contributed prompt in your own work, please credit BOTH the contributor and the library:

> [Contributor Name] (year). [Prompt title]. Contributed to *AI in Pathology Education — Companion Library* (Baba OZ, ed.). https://github.com/omarzbaba/AI_Pathology_Education

## Questions

If you're considering contributing but unsure whether your prompt fits, email Dr. Baba directly before submitting. We'd rather have a conversation than have you spend time on a submission that doesn't fit.
