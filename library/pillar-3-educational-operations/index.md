## Using AI to produce the artifacts around teaching events

Pillar III is the least glamorous and arguably the most valuable of the three. It covers the documentation that makes educational events work: the announcement, the schedule, the rubric, the evaluation form, the certificate, the post-event thank-you note. These are tasks where the *form* is well-understood, the *content* requires expert input, and the *time cost* of producing them from scratch is what kills well-intentioned educational programs.

The prompts here are organized by event type — pick the event you're running and walk through the relevant subsection. Each one assumes you bring the substantive content (objectives, faculty list, dates, your institutional vocabulary) and the model assembles the document.

A note on the **Companion App Builder** at the bottom of this page: it is a standalone, paste-into-Claude-Code prompt that lets another educator build an event-specific companion website like this one. It is the single most reusable artifact in this library — it productizes a complete capability into a single prompt.
