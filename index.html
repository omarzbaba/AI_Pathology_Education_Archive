A curated companion for the workshop

This library collects the prompts, worked examples, and operational templates introduced in the workshop — organized so you can return to them in the weeks and months after the session.

Every artifact here is opinionated, attribution-friendly, and built around three pillars: how to learn with AI, how to teach with AI, and how to use AI to produce the documentation that surrounds educational events.

The three pillars

Pillar I

Self-Education

Using AI to learn pathology — concept explanation, self-quizzing, algorithm walkthroughs, paper critique, multimodal practice.

Pillar II

Teaching
