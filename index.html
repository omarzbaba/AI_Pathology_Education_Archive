A curated companion — built to grow into a community

This library started as the companion to a workshop. The goal is for it to outgrow that origin: become the place where pathology educators come to find AI prompts that work, share the ones they've battle-tested, comment on what landed and what didn’t, and shape a discipline together.

Every artifact here is opinionated, attribution-friendly, and built around three pillars: how to learn with AI, how to teach with AI, and how to use AI to produce the documentation that surrounds educational events.

What this site does today — and where it’s going

Today

A curated library

88 battle-tested prompts across self-education, teaching, and educational operations — each with intent, expected output, common failure modes, and a required human verification step. Tutorials on writing good prompts, picking the right LLM, working with source-grounded AI safely. Worked examples showing real chains in practice.

Vision

A community of practice

Like Reddit, but focused: pathology educators sharing what worked, what didn’t, the tweaks that mattered, the failure modes they ran into. Submit your own prompts (reviewed and credited). Comment and upvote what others have contributed. Help shape the literacy we’ll all need as this technology becomes routine in how we teach and learn.

Features already shipped: floating Feedback button on every page, Submit-a-prompt form, comments + upvoting on each prompt, returning-visitor email shortcut, email notifications, admin moderation queue, privacy-first design (no tracking pixels, no third-party analytics, append-only access log).
